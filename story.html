OUR STORY

A Yemeni table, far from Yemen.

AZAL is a Yemeni restaurant for people who miss this food, and for people about to taste it for the first time.

THE MAP

Where the food comes from

Yemen’s cooking changes with its land: rice and slow-roasted meat in Hadramaut, stone-pot stews in the highlands around Sana’a, spiced tea in the port of Aden, fruit from the Red Sea plain. Our menu follows that map.

HOW WE COOK

Slowly, and for sharing.

Most of these dishes cannot be rushed. We cook them the long way, serve them on plates meant for the middle of the table, and keep the room calm enough to talk.

THE MAJLIS

A place kept for you.

In a Yemeni home, the majlis is the room where guests are welcomed. Majlis AZAL will be our membership for regulars: points on your orders, and a few things kept for our first members. It opens soon.
