## Rule that builds the guide from the cart (max 3 lines, never more)
1. If the order has a stew (Saltah 5 or Fahsa 4), line 1 is the stew line, because it is eaten hot and first.
2. If it has a rice platter (1, 2) or Haneeth (3), the next line is the platter line.
3. If it has bread and sahawiq (6) and line 1 is not already about bread, it goes in as the bread line.
4. The last line is the drink line if there is chai (8) or juice (9); otherwise the closing line.
5. A single small item (only hummus, only a drink) gets no guide. It would be noise.
Language follows the site language. FR uses U+00A0 before `: ; ? !`.

## Example (Sufra for 4: 1 lamb mandi, 1 chicken mandi, 1 saltah, 2 bread, 1 hummus, 4 chai)
1. Start with the stew while it still bubbles: tear the bread and scoop, no spoon needed.
2. Put the platter in the middle of the table; everyone eats from the same plate.
3. Finish with Adeni chai, poured hot, and take your time.
(The bread line is skipped because line 1 already covers bread.)
